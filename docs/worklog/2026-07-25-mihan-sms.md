# Mihan SMS provider for OTP

- **Date:** 2026-07-25
- **Area:** auth
- **Branch / commits:** `main` — `e40ae44` (uncommitted changes)
- **Status:** implementation complete, not deployed

## Goal
Use Mihan SMS Center as the default OTP SMS service. Its SOAP `send` operation receives the
complete message directly and does not use a template parameter.

## What changed
- `src/Auth/Sms/MihanSmsSender.cs` — posts XML-safe SOAP envelopes to Mihan with the final OTP
  message, phone number, sender number, and provider credentials.
- `src/Auth/Sms/SmsOptions.cs` and `src/Auth/Program.cs` — add Mihan settings and select its sender
  by default, while retaining relay, msgway, Kavenegar, and log implementations.
- `deploy/docker-compose.server.yml`, `deploy/docker-compose.newserver.yml`, and
  `deploy/docker-compose.server.edge.yml` — select Mihan and inject its settings through
  environment variables.
- `docs/ai/OPERATIONS.md` — records the required secret variable names without their values.

## Decisions
- Provider credentials are only read from `MIHAN_SMS_USERNAME` and `MIHAN_SMS_PASSWORD`; they are
  not committed to source or documentation.
- Development remains on the `log` provider so local OTP work does not deliver real messages.
